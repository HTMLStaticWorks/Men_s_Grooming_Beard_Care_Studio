The Executive Haircut & Beard Sculpt

Our most requested comprehensive service. Seamlessly combining haircut styling, structural beard sculpting, hot essential towel prep, and clean straight razor detailing.

Service Overview

The Executive is engineered for gentlemen who refuse to compromise on their appearance. Rather than treating hair and beard as separate components, your master barber harmonizes both into a unified aesthetic balance.

We begin with a scalp and beard bone structure analysis to determine where weight should be removed and where jawline strength needs amplification.

Who This Service Is For

  • • Professionals preparing for major corporate presentations, photoshoots, or media events.
  • • Men maintaining a medium-to-full beard who require precise jawline definition.
  • • Anyone seeking a comprehensive, unhurried grooming experience with premium scalp care.

The Step-by-Step Grooming Process

01

Aesthetic Diagnosis & Scalp Prep

Thorough examination of growth patterns, followed by a warm tea-tree scalp rinse.

02

Scissor & Clipper Haircut

Architectural cutting and seamless taper fading customized to head shape.

03

Beard Sculpting & Density Blending

Removal of stray whiskers, mustache trimming, and bottom line tapering.

04

Hot Towel Steam & Straight Razor Finish

Steamed towel wrapped over cheeks and neck, followed by razor sharp perimeter lines.

05

Apothecary Finishing & Blow Styling

Application of sandalwood beard oil, matte styling clay, and cooling aftershave splash.

Formulations Applied During This Service

Signature Beard Oil Cold-pressed jojoba & sandalwood for beard softness.
Matte Clay Styling Paste Bentonite clay for pliable matte texture.
