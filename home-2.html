Five-Stage Journey

The Iron & Ivory Ritual Protocol

From the moment you cross our threshold to the final mirror check, every step is calculated for relaxation and immaculate style.

STAGE 01

Consultation & Assessment

We examine your hair growth whorls, skull topography, and facial structure over a fresh espresso before making the first cut.

STAGE 02

Scalp Cleanse & Pre-Wash

Warm water rinse with organic tea-tree shampoo cleanses scalp buildup and relaxes facial tension.

STAGE 03

Precision Architectural Cut

Hand-forged steel scissor work creating crisp gradients, seamless blends, and effortless natural drape.

STAGE 04

Beard Sculpt & Razor Detail

Straight razor perimeter line-up accompanied by hot essential oil steam towels and botanical conditioning.

STAGE 05

Styling & Apothecary Finishing

Application of custom clay or beard balm tailored to your day's schedule, leaving you sharp and rejuvenated.
