Beard & Skin Elixir

Signature Beard Oil

$38.00 / 50ml (1.7 fl oz)

A fast-absorbing, non-greasy elixir formulated to nourish the facial skin beneath your beard while softening coarse whiskers with subtle notes of Australian sandalwood and cedarwood.

Master Barber Tip

"Apply 3–5 drops immediately after a warm shower while facial skin pores are open. Work upwards from the neck to the roots before combing down."

Inquire / Reserve at Studio Member 15% Off
Available for purchase during your next studio reservation or walk-in.

Key Benefits

  • Eliminates beard itch & dry flaky skin
  • Promotes natural beard follicle shine
  • Tames unruly flyaways without weight
  • Subtle masculine scent of woods & earth

Organic Ingredients

Simmondsia Chinensis (Jojoba) Seed Oil, Argania Spinosa (Argan) Kernel Oil, Tocopherol (Natural Vitamin E), Santalum Album (Sandalwood) Oil, Cedrus Atlantica (Cedarwood) Bark Extract.

Application Guide

  1. Dispense 3 to 5 drops into palm.
  2. Rub hands together to warm the botanicals.
  3. Massage deep into facial hair roots and skin.
  4. Comb through with a sandalwood beard comb.
