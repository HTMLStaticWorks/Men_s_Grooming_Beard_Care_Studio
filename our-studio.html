Heritage & Craft

Behind Iron & Ivory

Founded on the belief that a gentleman's haircut and beard trim should be an unhurried ritual of precision and restoration.

Our Philosophy

Craftsmanship Over Speed.

Iron & Ivory was established as an antidote to fast-casual haircut chains and overly noisy salon spaces. We restored the traditional dignity of the barbershop while integrating contemporary aesthetic standards.

Every barber in our studio is a career artisan who undergoes rigorous internal mastery training in head topography, face-shape proportion balance, and straight razor ergonomics.
